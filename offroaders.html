HOW TO PLAY

Goal: Finish each race as high as you can. Cash and championship points are awarded by finishing position.

Keyboard: your truck auto-accelerates — ←→/AD steer, Space nitro, ↓/S brake, Esc pause.

The whole track is on screen at once — no scrolling, so you can always see the pack and plan your line.

Touch: Your truck accelerates automatically. Push the steering pad (bottom-right) in the direction you want the truck to go — push down to head down the screen. NITRO is the button on the left.

Drifting: In Enhanced mode the trucks turn normally — but hold NITRO while steering and the truck automatically drifts through the corner.

Jumps: Hit the ramps (chevrons) with speed to launch — you can't steer much in the air, so line it up before takeoff and brace for the landing.

Tips: The track has edge walls so you can't fly off into the grass — lean on them through corners. Save nitro for straights. Spend winnings in the Garage on Engine, Tires (grip), Shocks and Nitro. Switch between Enhanced (2.5D) and Classic graphics with the Garage button.
