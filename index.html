HOW TO PLAY

Goal: Finish each race as high as you can. Cash and championship points are awarded by finishing position.

Keyboard: ↑/W gas, ↓/S brake, ←→/AD steer, Space nitro, Esc pause.

Touch: Use the on-screen d-pad on the right — push up to go (farther = faster), down to brake, left/right to steer. The NITRO button is on the left.

Drifting: In Enhanced mode the trucks turn normally — but hold NITRO while turning and the truck automatically drifts through the corner.

Jumps: Hit the ramps (chevrons) with speed to launch — you can't steer much in the air, so line it up before takeoff and brace for the landing.

Tips: Stay on the dirt track — grass slows you down. Save nitro for straights. Spend winnings in the Garage on Engine, Tires (grip), Shocks and Nitro. Switch between Enhanced (2.5D) and Classic graphics with the Garage button.
